Authentic Japanese Ramen

Description

A globally celebrated comfort food featuring bouncy, springy wheat noodles nestled in a deeply savory, slow-simmered broth, topped with tender protein and a perfect soft-boiled egg.

This dish brings the comforting, minimalist atmosphere of a traditional Tokyo ramen shop straight to your dining table.

Ingredients

  • 1 pack of fresh ramen noodles
  • 2 cups of rich chicken or pork broth
  • 1 tablespoon of soy sauce
  • 1 soft-boiled egg (marinated if possible)
  • 2 slices of cooked pork belly or chicken
  • 1 tablespoon of chopped green onions

Steps

  1. Bring your savory broth and soy sauce to a gentle simmer in a small pot over medium heat.
  2. In a separate pot of boiling water, cook your fresh ramen noodles for exactly 2-3 minutes until bouncy, then drain them.
  3. Place the cooked noodles neatly into your favorite serving bowl.
  4. Carefully pour the hot, steaming broth directly over the noodles.
  5. Arrange your pork slices, soft-boiled egg halves, and a generous handful of green onions beautifully on top.
