Croissant au Chocolat

Description

A luxurious French pastry featuring flaky, golden layers of buttery dough wrapped around rich, dark chocolate bars.

Perfect for a sophisticated morning breakfast or paired with a hot espresso in the afternoon.

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet of high-quality puff pastry
  • 100g of dark chocolate sticks or bars
  • 1 egg (for the egg wash)
  • 1 tablespoon of milk

Steps

  1. Roll out your puff pastry sheet on a clean surface.
  2. Cut the pastry into neat triangles or rectangles.
  3. Place a piece of dark chocolate at the wide edge of the pastry.
  4. Carefully roll the pastry up tightly around the chocolate.
  5. Whisk the egg and milk together, then brush it over the top of the croissants.
  6. Bake in a preheated oven at 200°C (400°F) for 15-20 minutes until golden brown.
